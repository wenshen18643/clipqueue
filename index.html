ClipQueue

ClipQueue is a personal desktop tool that uploads short video clips I edit to my own accounts, at times I choose: YouTube Shorts, TikTok (sent to my TikTok inbox as a draft, which I finish and post in the TikTok app) and Instagram Reels.

It runs only on my own computer and signs in with each platform's official OAuth. It asks only for the permissions needed to upload: youtube.upload on YouTube, video.upload on TikTok, and Instagram content publishing. It does not read, change or delete anything else on these accounts.

It is not offered to other users.
